title: "Storage and the 3-2-1 rule"
description: "Which copies 3to1go covers, and where Station can keep your snapshots."
---

The [3-2-1 rule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backup#Storage) says to keep **3** copies of your data, on **2** different media, with **1** of them offsite. 3to1go gives you **two** copies: your live files and Station's snapshots. The third copy is always up to you.

| Copy | What | Covered by |
|------|------|------------|
| 1: live data | The original files on your machine | Scout |
| 2: second copy, different media | Encrypted snapshots on a separate machine | **3to1go: Scout → Station** |
| 3: third copy | An independent copy of Station's snapshots | Your choice (see below) |

## Getting the third copy

Copy Station's backup folder somewhere independent of Station. Where Station runs decides which copy is your offsite one:

<Warning>
  Running Station offsite does **not** give you three copies on its own. If Station's disk fails at the same time as a machine, there's nothing else to restore from.
</Warning>

Station only holds encrypted archives, so it can run on hardware you don't fully control, and its folder can be copied anywhere without exposing your files.

## Where Station can store snapshots

Station writes ordinary files into its backup folder (`/backups` in the container, set by `BACKUP_DIR` in Compose). Any tool that can replicate a folder can make your third copy.

- **Local disk.** The default.
- **Mounted NAS or removable drive.** Mount it on the Docker host, give Station write access, and point `BACKUP_DIR` at it.
- **Dropbox, Google Drive, or OneDrive.** Point `BACKUP_DIR` at the local sync folder. The provider only sees encrypted archives.
- **Object storage.** There's no native backend. Copy the backup folder with an external tool. If you use a filesystem mount over object storage instead, check that it supports writes, listing, renames, and deletion first.

Keep the Scout keys separately from these copies. To recover Station itself, not just the archives, also keep its signing key and PostgreSQL database. See [Install Station](/station/install#what-gets-created). A mirror may also copy retention deletions, depending on the sync tool's settings.
